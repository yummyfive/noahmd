La nostra storia

A little piece
of Naples.

Our story began in the heart of Naples, where our founder, Antonio Bellini, grew up surrounded by the aromas of fresh tomatoes, wood-fired ovens and his grandmother's kitchen.

In 1987, he brought that tradition to Casa Bellini. Today, we continue to honour the simple Italian belief that great food begins with great ingredients and a little love.
